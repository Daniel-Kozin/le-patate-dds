      💀 Eviatar mode

      Everything from Hard mode, plus:

      • ⏰
        The exam clock: you start with 30 seconds. Every merge adds time (bigger merges add more) and right answers add a lot. At zero: pens down, game over.
      • 📏
        A narrow board: it starts at about two-thirds width. A wrong answer squeezes it a lot, a right answer widens it a little.
      • ⚡
        A quiz every 15 balls, 10 seconds to answer, and a ball drops every 2 seconds while you think.
      • ❌
        Wrong: −100, your biggest character drops two levels, and you fail the course for 15 seconds.
      • 👀
        Questions about your board: "How many Omers are on the board?" You get 5 seconds.
      • 🧠
        Recite the lecture: 5 faces flash, then tap them in the same order in the strip under the board.
      • ⌨️
        Type the word: a nasty long word pops up. Type it exactly before the timer runs out (half a second per letter, plus 1.5 seconds). Wrong or too slow counts as a wrong answer.
      • 🎲
        Random minigames (the game keeps running!): 🧩 Who's this?, 🃏 Memory pairs, ⏱️ The exam starts NOW (tap on green, not before), ☕ Coffee break (tap fast for turbo drops) and 🔍 Find the mistake (tap the wrong line of a derivation).
      • 💣
        Bomb ball: merge it within 15 seconds or it blows up everything it touches.
      • 🧱
        Midterm wall: every 20 balls, a bar blocks the middle of the board for the next 35 balls.
      • 🕶️
        He's watching: don't drop for 5 seconds and Eviatar drops one for you.
      • 📝
        Final exam every 100 balls: 3 questions in a row. All right = big bonus. One wrong = you lose your biggest character.
      • ✖️2
        All points are doubled.

      • ⏳

      • 🐢
        A turtle bubble pops up at random. Tap it within 3 seconds or your biggest character gets deleted.
      • 📚
